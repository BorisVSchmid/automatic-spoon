In VR (Meta Quest browser)

  • Real size: 1 m in VR is 1 m in the room, at your own eye height.
  • You start just inside the door; walk around for real. Clear the floor first: the VR walls are not there.
  • Colour wheel on your left hand: point with the right controller and pull the trigger to pick a hue, then the bar beside it to set how light or dark it is.
  • Then point at the floor, the tiles or a plain wall and pull the trigger to repaint it. The small button under the wheel restores the original colours.
